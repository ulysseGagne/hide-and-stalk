# Hide & Seek — Campus Edition (Université Laval)

A campus-wide game of hide-and-seek, played on foot, in which the seekers find a hidden player by asking questions. Provisional draft.

## Concept

The game begins as the hide-and-seek everyone knows: one player hides, the others look. Two things make this version different.

- The board is the entire Université Laval campus, and everyone moves on foot.
- The seekers do not search blindly. They find the hider by asking questions from a fixed menu — anything from "north or south of us?" to "which café is nearest you?" to a quick photo of the surroundings — and the hider must answer every one truthfully.

Each answer rules out part of the campus, until the seekers have narrowed it down enough to walk straight to the hider and catch them.

## The website

Questions are powerful, so they cannot be unlimited — otherwise the hider would be found in seconds. A small GitHub Pages site keeps the game paced and fair. It:

- offers three questions at a time, drawn at random, and lets the seekers pick one to ask;
- releases the next set only after a short delay, so the seekers must spend that time searching rather than asking non-stop;
- assembles the message to send, including the seekers' current location;
- runs the clock, and at the catch reports how long the hider lasted and a recap of the questions asked.

## Goal

Players take turns hiding; the longest single hide wins. A round lasts roughly 15 to 30 minutes.

## Answering

Answers are sent by text. Google Maps supplies any spatial answer — shared location, the Measure-distance tool, or a "… near me" search. The website handles the draw, the timer, the location, and the recap.

## Questions (provisional)

### Direction and distance, relative to the seekers
1. North or south of the seekers?
2. East or west of the seekers?
3. In front of or behind the seekers (per the Maps orientation cone)?
4. Walking time from the seekers to the hider, in minutes?
5. Within 100 / 200 / 300 / 500 m of the seekers (straight-line)?

### Nearest of a kind
6. Which pavillon?
7. Which café?
8. Which parking lot?
9. Which bus stop?
10. Which street?
11. Which tall building?
12. Which àVélo station?

### Landmarks — closer to it than the seekers?
13. Pavillon Casault (the tower)?
14. The Université Laval sign?
15. The TELUS stadium?
16. The athletics track?
17. The twin towers?
18. The petite maison?
19. The Grand Axe intersection?
20. The golf course?
21. The greenhouses?
22. The volleyball court?
23. The Pub U?
24. The outer ring of campus?

### Known without a tool
25. Inside or outside?
26. Which floor?
27. Nearest room number?

### Photo, framed to reveal as little as possible
28. The tallest thing in sight.
29. The sky, straight up.
30. The nearest door or sign.
31. A tightly cropped selfie.
32. The hider's feet and the ground.
33. The ceiling overhead.
34. The floor's texture.
35. A window, from inside or from outside.

### Observation
36. Visible on Google Street View?
37. A bike lane in sight?
38. How many people are in sight?
39. How many buildings are in sight?
40. How many trees are in sight?
41. A parked car in sight?
42. Sun on the hider right now?
43. Grass in sight?
44. A construction site in sight?
45. A bench in sight?
