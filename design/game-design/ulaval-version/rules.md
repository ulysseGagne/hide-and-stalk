## A round

| Time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Start | Anyone on the team presses Start, once the whole team is in its Discord call |
| 0:00 to 10:00 | The hider walks to a spot. The stalkers wait where the round started |
| 10:00 | The hunt starts: question 1. The hider freezes |
| Every 5 min | A new question: three cards, the stalkers send one, the hider answers |
| Up to 40:00 | Question 7 would come: the hider wins. A scan of the hider's code before that: the stalkers win |

A round is **40 minutes at most**: 10 to hide, 30 to find. It ends sooner when
the hider is found. Everyone takes a turn hiding (Play again picks whoever has
hidden least); the longest hide of the day wins.

## The eight rules, as the app shows them

**Stalkers win if they find the hider within 30 minutes.**

1. **Hide in plain sight.** On campus, within 20 m of a path, where people on it could see you.
2. **Walk, then freeze.** No running. Once the hunt starts, don't move.
3. **Stalkers can run.** Outside only: indoors, everyone walks.
4. **Nothing underground.** No tunnels, no basements, for anyone.
5. **Touch nothing.** Don't open, move or climb anything.
6. **Answer truthfully.** Right away, from where you are.
7. **Found?** Show your code. Stalkers win.
8. **Discord call.** Stay in it all game. Hider: on mute.

Most important first. The two the app underlines in red: 1 and 2.

## The fine print

What a player sees when they tap a rule on the how-to-play page.

**1. Hide in plain sight.**
- Campus is what the app's map shows. Everything past its edge is out: Place Sainte-Foy, Place Laurier, the streets across the boulevards.
- A path is anywhere people walk: a sidewalk, a trail, a plaza, a parking lot, a hallway, an atrium.
- In plain sight: someone walking along that path would see you at some point. Sit, stand, blend into a crowd. Not inside, under or behind anything that hides you: a bush, a closed door, a stall, a coat over your head.
- Public places only: anywhere you can walk into without a key, a card or a ticket. Not classrooms, offices, labs, washrooms, residences, rooftops or construction sites. A locked building is not public.
- Cafés, cafeterias and atriums are fine.

**2. Walk, then freeze.**
- You have 10 minutes to walk to your spot. No running, no bike, scooter, bus or car.
- You can change your mind while the 10 minutes last.
- From question 1 to the end of the round, stay put. Sitting down, standing up or turning around is fine; anything that could change an answer is not.
- Not at your spot when the hunt starts? Where you are is your spot.
- Need to leave your spot (cold, washroom, anything)? Say so in the call: an organizer can pause your team.

**3. Stalkers can run.**
- Split up or stick together, your call. "North or south of me?" and every "…than me?" question are about whoever sends it.
- Indoors, walk and keep it down: people are working.
- While the hider hides, wait where the round started and don't watch where they go.
- The app is your only way to find the hider: no Snap Map, no Find My, no asking their friends.

**4. Nothing underground.**
- GPS doesn't work underground, and the app runs on it: the stalkers' positions draw the questions on the map, and the hider's give the coordinates and the heatmap.
- That means the tunnels between pavilions, basement floors and underground parking.
- Stalkers too: no shortcuts through the tunnels.
- Not sure if it counts? Go up a floor.

**5. Touch nothing.**
- Public doors you walk through are fine: building entrances, hallway doors.
- Not fine: getting inside or behind anything you have to open (a closet, a locker, a cabinet, a room's door), moving anything to hide (chairs, bins, signs), climbing anything (trees, walls, railings, roofs).

**6. Answer truthfully.**
- Answer as soon as the question comes in: about a minute, two for a photo. Stalling is cheating.
- Not sure? Give your honest best answer. Got one wrong? Fix it with Change, right away.
- "North or south of me?", "Within 200 m of me?", "Closer to … than I am?": go by the line, the circle or the place drawn on your map. It's drawn from where the stalker was when they sent the question.
- "Which … are you closest to?": as the crow flies, from where you really are. The app lists the options nearest first.
- Inside means inside a building's walls. A covered walkway or a bus shelter is outside.
- Photos: taken now, from your spot. Frame them to give away as little as you can, but what the question asks for has to be in the picture. Nothing like it around? Answer N/A.

**7. Found? Show your code.**
- A stalker has to come up to you and scan the code on your phone (it's on your QUESTIONS tab). Seeing you from far away doesn't count.
- Spotted? Don't run, and don't hide your screen.
- The scan won't work? The stalker presses "Hider has been found", inside the scanner.
- Spotted another team's hider? Walk on and say nothing. Their code won't work for you anyway.
- 30 minutes of hunting and nobody has scanned you: you win.

**8. Discord call.**
- Each team has its own voice channel. Everyone joins it before pressing Start.
- The hider listens, on mute: you'll hear them coming, they won't hear a thing.
- Earbuds help. So does a charged phone.

## Rulings, for the organizers

- **A rule broken by mistake** (the hider 30 m from a path, an answer that was wrong): fix it and keep playing. If it decided the round, the round doesn't count for longest hide; play it again.
- **Pause.** From the admin board: an emergency, a washroom, a lost player. Players can't pause.
- **Someone leaves.** Take them off the team on the admin board, so Play again never picks them to hide.
- **Ties for longest hide.** Every hider who lasted the full 30:00 shares it. (The results list puts the first one to finish on top; that order means nothing.)
- **Questions the app can't settle** (is this a path? is this a basement?): the organizer's call, and the call holds for everyone the rest of the day. Note it here afterwards.

## Why each rule exists

1. **Plain sight.** The questions narrow the hider down to a few dozen metres; the last metres are found by eye. A hider nobody could see from anywhere makes that impossible, and the places that hide you completely (closets, bushes, stalls) are the ones nobody should be in. The original game's spot rule is the same idea: public, next to a path on the map.
2. **Freeze.** The hints map is every answer laid over each other. A hider who moves makes earlier answers false, and the map lies to the stalkers. The hider also sees every stalker's pin on their map, so the urge to slip away is real: it needs saying.
3. **Run outside, walk inside.** Stalkers running is the fun; running in hallways is how someone gets hurt. The hider never runs, so 10 minutes reach the same distance for everyone.
4. **Underground.** "…than me?" questions are drawn from the stalker's GPS fix when they send it, and the coordinates and heatmap questions from the hider's. Underground, both are wrong.
5. **Touch nothing.** Nobody damages anything, nobody climbs, nobody has to explain themselves to campus security.
6. **Truthfully, right away.** Truthful answers are the whole game. Right away because a question only lasts five minutes: a slow answer eats the stalkers' time.
7. **The code.** The catch is face to face. Nothing about proximity ends a round, so a bad GPS fix can never cost anyone the game.
8. **The call.** Stalkers who split up still hunt together, and organizers can hop into any team's channel. The hider on mute means nothing reaches the stalkers but the answers.

## Changed from the earlier card

- The goal line says 30 minutes instead of "before question 7".
- New: freeze (it was in the concept, never on the card), on campus, in plain sight (it was only in the hiding screen's text), indoors everyone walks, basements, the hider on mute.
- "One hider. 10 min to hide, a question every 5 min." is off the list: the app's timers say it, and the how-to-play page shows it.
- The red underlines moved from "near a path" and "touch nothing" to "plain sight" and "freeze".

## Still open

- **The hider on mute.** Without it, the hider can chat with the team, and the background noise of a café can give them away (fun, or unfair).
- **Basements.** "No tunnels" was the rule; basements are added because many pavilions' basement hallways are the tunnels. Too strict for some buildings?
- **Libraries.** Allowed (public, plain sight), as long as everyone walks and keeps it down. Ban them instead?
- **Ties** for longest hide: share it, or break it (fewest questions asked? who was closest to being found?).
