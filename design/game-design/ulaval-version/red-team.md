# Red team: the game

Oct 3, 2026, two days before the event. This is about the game, not the app:
what players will try, what it would do to a round, and what stops it. The rules
it refers to are in [`rules.md`](rules.md).

## The short version

1. **One test stops almost every bad hiding spot.** "A stalker walking along a path would see you, and could walk right up to you, without opening, moving or climbing anything." Lockers, stalls, classrooms, bushes, trees and roofs all fail it, and nobody has to remember a list. A second test covers what is left: "somewhere you could sit for 40 minutes without anyone finding it odd" rules out stairwells, the space under a staircase and behind a vending machine.
2. **Freezing is what makes one shared call work.** In Dream's Manhunt the runner can use what he overhears. Here the hider can't move, so hearing every plan gives them nothing but the tension. The call costs the stalkers nothing, and costs the hider only the noise around them.
3. **The hider is most tempted to move exactly when the game is best.** They watch the stalkers' pins close in on their map. The freeze rule says so out loud, and says why.
4. **Making every stalker agree on a question forces them to talk,** and the hider hears every argument. The risk is a stalker whose phone is locked or dead blocking the team, so the app only waits for stalkers who have had it open in the last 2 minutes.
5. **What is left to watch on the day:** big pavilions searched floor by floor, many hiders tied at 30:00, hiders getting cold, and runners near roads and other people.

## How to read the tables

What stops it: **Rule n** (a rule says no), **Allowed** (it's clever: the game
working as meant), **App** (the app does it), **Ruling** (an organizer settles
it on the day), **Watch** (it waits for a playtest), **Open** (yours to decide).

## Where the hider can be

| What a hider tries | What it does to the game | What stops it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A locker, a closet, a washroom stall, a classroom | The end of the round becomes opening doors one by one | Rule 1: nothing to open |
| A room with its door wide open, seen from the hallway | Not a path, the door can close, a class can start | Rule 1: no rooms at all |
| Behind a pillar, a vending machine, under a table or a staircase, in a bush | Seen from one exact spot at best | Rule 1: seen from the path; odd to sit there for 40 minutes |
| Up a tree, on a roof, on a ledge | Nobody can walk up to scan the code; unsafe | Rule 1: walk right up to you |
| A hood up, other clothes, face behind a book | The last metres become impossible | Rule 1: look like yourself |
| A crowded cafeteria at 1 PM | Visible and recognizable, hard to spot | Allowed: clever |
| A seat right between two cafés, under an overhang, on the seam of two pavilions | Every honest answer stays vague | Allowed: this is the hider's whole game |
| The 4th floor of a big pavilion | The map is flat: a building can take a long search, floor by floor | Watch. The deck has floor and room-number cards. Fallback: "indoors, only on a floor with a door to the outside" |
| A stairwell landing, an elevator | A hideout, not a place people stay | Rule 1 (named) |
| A library | Seen from the aisles, but the hider couldn't talk (rule 8), and running stalkers disturb people working | Rule 1 (named). Open |
| A store's aisles | Shelves hide you; staff wonder | Rule 1 (named) |
| 20 m into the woods off a trail | Not seen from the trail | Rule 1: the test |
| A parked car, a bus at the terminal | Inside something; it can leave | Rule 1 and rule 3 |
| A tunnel, a basement, underground parking | GPS is wrong there, so the map is wrong | Rule 4 |
| Just past the campus edge | Out of the play area | Rule 1: the red line |
| Right next to where the round started | A bluff: the stalkers may never look | Allowed: clever |

## The hider moving

| What a hider tries | What it does to the game | What stops it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Running to the far end of campus | The hunt becomes a commute | Rule 2 |
| A bike, a scooter, a bus | Same | Rule 2 |
| Sliding away when the pins on their map get close | Every earlier answer now lies | Rule 3, which says the temptation out loud |
| A few steps per question, never coming back | A slow drift | Rule 3: then back to your spot |
| Standing in a line that moves | Moving without meaning to | Rule 1 (odd for 40 minutes) and rule 3 |
| Has to move: washroom, cold, security asks | — | Ruling: tell the call, an organizer pauses the team |

## The answers

| What a hider tries | What it does to the game | What stops it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technically true ("a covered walkway is inside") | Annoying; the map lies a little | Rule 5: the way a stalker next to you would. Inside is defined |
| Taking three minutes to answer | Eats the stalkers' 5 minutes | Rule 6 |
| Changing an answer late to throw them off | The map lies | Rule 5: Change is for mistakes, right away |
| A blurry photo, a 2 cm crop, an old photo | Useless answer | Rule 5: now, from your spot, clear enough to recognize |
| A bluff in the call ("I'm in a café") | Banter | Allowed: only the app's answers count |
| Using the stalkers' pins to answer "…than me?" | That's how those questions work | Allowed |

## The stalkers

| What stalkers try | What it does to the game | What stops it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Watching where the hider walks off | Skips the game | Rule 2 (fine print) |
| Moving somewhere central during the 10 minutes | Breaks "they could walk there in 10 minutes" | Rule 2: wait where the round started |
| Snap Map, Find My, friends, other teams, people nearby | Skips the game | Rule 8: no outside help |
| Calling the hider's phone to make it ring | Cuts the hider out of the call; skips the app | Rule 8: no calling or texting |
| Shouting, then listening for their own voice in the hider's mic | Works only within earshot, so only at the very end | Allowed: what you hear is fair game. Open |
| Pressing "Hider has been found" from across the hall | A false catch | Rule 7: only standing next to the hider |
| One stalker sends a question on their own | The team loses a question it never discussed | App: every stalker picks the same card first |
| One stalker refuses to agree | Their own team loses time; the questions wait in hand, they are not lost | Clever or annoying? Annoying, and to their own team |
| A stalker's phone is locked or dead | Would block every question | App: only stalkers who had it open in the last 2 minutes count |
| Saving questions, then sending two "…than me?" from opposite ends of campus | Two cuts at once | Allowed: clever |
| Running in hallways and across roads | Safety, and the club's name on campus | Not in the rules, by choice. Say it at the briefing |

## The call

| What happens | What it does to the game | What stops it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The hider hears the stalkers' plans | Nothing they can use: they can't move | Allowed: the point of the call |
| The hider mutes to hide the noise around them | Takes the hider out of the game | Rule 8: unmuted, the hider too |
| The café, the wind, an announcement behind the hider | A clue | Allowed: fair game |
| Several stalkers' phones on speaker next to each other | Echo, feedback | Earbuds (rule 8, and the page's "Bring") |
| A hider who doesn't talk | A quieter game, nothing worse | Nothing to stop: rule 8 can only invite |

## The round itself

| What happens | What it does to the game | What stops it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Many hiders last the full 30:00 | The day's winner is a tie | Ruling: they share it. Open: a tie-break |
| Teams of different sizes | A hide against one stalker isn't a hide against four | Make teams splits evenly. Ruling: latecomers go onto the smallest teams (drag them on the admin board) |
| Sitting still 30 minutes outside in October | The hider gets cold and wants to move | The page's "Bring" says a warm layer |
| A phone at 10% | A dead hider phone stops the round | The page's "Bring" says charged; ruling for when it happens |
| Two teams' hiders in the same spot | A stalker finds the wrong hider | Their code won't scan; rule 7: walk on |
| Five minutes between questions with nothing new to go on | Stalkers search with what they have | Fine: unsent questions wait in hand, and the next one comes face up the moment one is sent |
