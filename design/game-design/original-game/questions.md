===============================================================================
JET LAG: HIDE + SEEK  --  EXHAUSTIVE QUESTION CATALOG
===============================================================================

This is the complete question menu for the canonical Home Game, drawn from the
community master list (jetlag.denull.ru/en/rules/questions/) and cross-checked
against the Japan / NYC / Switzerland episode transcripts. The Japan (Large)
episode calls this "62 questions in our giant question menu"; the exact count of
AVAILABLE questions depends on game size (see the availability tags below), so
the master catalog here lists MORE than 62 -- you enable the subset for your
size. For rules on how questions work, answer times, and re-asking, see rules.txt
sections 6-8. For Quebec-City localization/bans, see rules.txt section 13.4.

LEGEND
  [S] available in Small games   [M] Medium   [L] Large
  "[S][M][L]" = all sizes; "[M][L]" = medium and large only; etc.
  Draw/keep = how many Hider Deck cards the Hider draws and keeps for answering
  a question of that category (this is the Seekers' "cost" -- it arms the Hider).

CARD-DRAW BY CATEGORY (repeat of rules.txt section 8, for convenience)
  Matching ....... Draw 3, keep 1
  Measuring ...... Draw 3, keep 1
  Radar .......... Draw 2, keep 1
  Thermometer .... Draw 2, keep 1
  Photo .......... Draw 1, keep 1 (blind -- top card, unseen)
  Tentacles ...... Draw 4, keep 2
  RE-ASK penalty: asking an already-asked question doubles the draw (2nd ask),
  triples it (3rd ask). Prefer fresh questions.

CATEGORY 1 -- MATCHING          (Yes/No: is this attribute the SAME as ours?)
Draw 3, keep 1.   Available: all sizes unless noted.
===============================================================================
Matching asks whether a specific attribute of the Hider's location is the SAME
as (or DIFFERENT from) the Seekers'. Answer is a plain yes/no. Best for slicing
the map along administrative or network lines.

  TRANSIT
    1.  Commercial Airport      -- same nearest commercial airport?          [S][M][L]
    2.  Transit Line            -- same transit line (share a line)?         [M][L]
    3.  Station's Name Length   -- same number of letters in station name?   [M][L]
    4.  Street or Path          -- same nearest street/path name?            [L]

  ADMINISTRATIVE DIVISIONS  (map these to your country's levels; see 13.4)
    5.  1st Administrative Division -- same (e.g. state/province/prefecture)? [S][M][L]
    6.  2nd Administrative Division -- same (e.g. county/RCM)?               [S][M][L]
    7.  3rd Administrative Division -- same (e.g. municipality/borough)?     [M][L]
    8.  4th Administrative Division -- same (finest sub-division)?           [L]

  NATURAL
    9.  Mountain                -- same nearest named mountain?              [S][M][L]
    10. Landmass                -- same landmass/island?                     [L]
    11. Park                    -- same nearest park?                        [S][M][L]

  PLACES OF INTEREST
    12. Amusement Park          -- same nearest amusement park?             [M][L]
    13. Zoo                     -- same nearest zoo?                        [M][L]
    14. Aquarium                -- same nearest aquarium?                   [M][L]
    15. Golf Course             -- same nearest golf course?               [M][L]
    16. Museum                  -- same nearest museum?                     [S][M][L]
    17. Movie Theater           -- same nearest movie theater?             [S][M][L]

  PUBLIC UTILITIES
    18. Hospital                -- same nearest hospital?                   [S][M][L]
    19. Library                 -- same nearest library?                    [S][M][L]
    20. Foreign Consulate       -- same nearest foreign consulate?          [L]

  In play (Japan): "is his nearest Shinkansen [high-speed] line the same as
  ours?" and "same prefecture?" were pivotal Matching questions. (NYC): "same
  borough?" and "same nearest commercial airport (LaGuardia)?"

CATEGORY 2 -- MEASURING         (Closer / Further: vs. us, nearer or farther?)
Draw 3, keep 1.   Available: all sizes unless noted.
===============================================================================
Measuring asks whether the Hider is CLOSER TO or FURTHER FROM a feature than the
Seekers are. Answer is "closer" or "further." Best for drawing a perpendicular
bisector across the map (everything on the Hider's side stays in play).

  TRANSIT-RELATED
    1.  Commercial Airport      -- closer to a commercial airport?           [S][M][L]
    2.  High-Speed Train Line   -- closer to a high-speed rail line?         [L]
    3.  Rail Station            -- closer to a rail station?                 [M][L]

  BORDERS
    4.  International Border     -- closer to an international border?        [L]
    5.  1st Admin Division Border-- closer to a 1st-division border?         [M][L]
    6.  2nd Admin Division Border-- closer to a 2nd-division border?         [M][L]

  NATURAL
    7.  Sea Level               -- closer to sea level (i.e. lower)?         [S][M][L]
    8.  Body of Water           -- closer to a body of water?               [S][M][L]
    9.  Coastline               -- closer to a coastline?                   [M][L]
    10. Mountain                -- closer to a mountain?                     [S][M][L]
    11. Park                    -- closer to a park?                         [S][M][L]

  PLACES OF INTEREST
    12. Amusement Park          -- closer to an amusement park?             [M][L]
    13. Zoo                     -- closer to a zoo?                         [M][L]
    14. Aquarium                -- closer to an aquarium?                   [M][L]
    15. Golf Course             -- closer to a golf course?                 [S][M][L]
    16. Museum                  -- closer to a museum?                      [S][M][L]
    17. Movie Theater           -- closer to a movie theater?              [S][M][L]

  PUBLIC UTILITIES
    18. Hospital                -- closer to a hospital?                    [S][M][L]
    19. Library                 -- closer to a library?                    [S][M][L]
    20. Foreign Consulate       -- closer to a foreign consulate?           [L]

  In play: (Switzerland) relative longitude/latitude to split the country E/W
  then N/S; relative elevation. (Japan) "closer to a Shinkansen line? a
  coastline?" (NYC) "closer to a hospital?" as an endgame pinpoint.
  NOTE: the Switzerland-era "Relative" category = today's Matching + Measuring
  merged (see rules.txt section 11).


===============================================================================
CATEGORY 3 -- RADAR             (Yes/No: are you WITHIN this distance of us?)
Draw 2, keep 1.
===============================================================================
Radar asks whether the Hider is within a chosen radius of the Seekers' current
position. Answer is hit (yes, within) or miss (no). Best for carving big circles
off the map early, or a tight circle to confirm the endgame.

  Available distances (enable the ones that fit your map/size):
    - 1/4 Mile        (~400 m)        endgame / precision
    - 1/2 Mile        (~800 m)
    - 1 Mile          (~1.6 km)
    - 3 Miles         (~4.8 km)
    - 5 Miles         (~8 km)
    - 10 Miles        (~16 km)
    - 25 Miles        (~40 km)
    - 50 Miles        (~80 km)
    - 100 Miles       (~160 km)
    - "Choose" / Freestyle -- pick ANY custom distance (used in NYC to cut a
      precise 4.2/4.3-mile circle around upper Manhattan).

  In play: (Japan) a 100-mile Radar first ("has he Shinkansen'd out of Tokyo?"),
  later a 50-mile then half-mile Radar to close in. Note the show reminded us "a
  Radar is worth two cards" -- i.e. draw 2 keep 1.


===============================================================================
CATEGORY 4 -- THERMOMETER       (Move, then: did we get HOTTER or COLDER?)
Draw 2, keep 1.
===============================================================================
The Seekers travel AT LEAST the chosen distance in a single direction, then ask
the Thermometer: did that movement bring them nearer ("hotter") or farther
("colder") from the Hider? Best tool for establishing a DIRECTION.

  Available distances by size:
    - 1/2 Mile      [S][M][L]
    - 3 Miles       [S][M][L]
    - 10 Miles      [M][L]
    - 50 Miles      [L]

  In play: (Japan) a half-mile Thermometer north of Tokyo confirmed "hotter =
  go north," the single most important early move. (NYC) they guarded their
  scarce Thermometers; the Hiders used Drained Brain to ban the easy half-mile
  Thermometer.


===============================================================================
CATEGORY 5 -- PHOTO             (Hider sends a picture of a named thing)
Draw 1, keep 1 (blind).   Answer time: 10 min (S/M) / 20 min (L).
===============================================================================
The Hider must photograph the named subject from within their zone. The Hider
frames to leak as little as possible (crop out mountains, pick a bland angle);
that is legal skill. Some photos allow blurring text. Best for incidental
"bleed-over" information and for the endgame.

  SMALL games:
    1.  Any Building Visible from Transit Station
    2.  Widest Street
    3.  Tree
    4.  Tallest Structure in Your Current Sightline
    5.  You (a selfie)
    6.  The Sky (straight up)

  MEDIUM & LARGE add:
    7.  Tallest Building Visible from Transit Station
    8.  Trace Nearest Street/Path  (a traced outline of the nearest street/path)
    9.  2 Buildings
    10. Restaurant Interior
    11. Park
    12. Grocery Store Aisle
    13. Place of Worship
    14. Train Platform

  LARGE only add:
    15. 1/2 Mile of Streets Traced  (walk ~1/2 mile making turns; send the map
        outline -- the "Strava map" the Japan seekers used to pinpoint Yamadera)
    16. Tallest Mountain Visible from Transit Station
    17. Biggest Body of Water in Your Zone
    18. 5 Buildings  (must include ground level and up to four stories)

  In play: "five buildings" is a recurring high-information request (Japan &
  Switzerland both used it -- the Swiss Hider dreaded it because he couldn't
  frame five buildings without revealing snow). "Tallest structure in sightline"
  and "photo of you" are common endgame pinpoints. The framing battle is central
  Hider skill: reveal nothing while telling the truth.


===============================================================================
CATEGORY 6 -- TENTACLES         (Which of these are you NEAREST to?)
Draw 4, keep 2.   NOT available in Small games.
===============================================================================
Tentacles only works when the Seekers are within the tool's radius of the Hider.
The Seekers name a category; the Hider must reveal which specific instance of
that category (in the radius) they are NEAREST to. Very powerful for closing out
a search in a dense area -- hence the highest card cost (draw 4, keep 2).

  MEDIUM games (1-mile radius):
    1.  Museums Within 1 Mile      -- which museum are you nearest?
    2.  Libraries Within 1 Mile
    3.  Movie Theaters Within 1 Mile
    4.  Hospitals Within 1 Mile

  LARGE games add (15-mile radius):
    5.  Metro Lines Within 15 Miles
    6.  Zoos Within 15 Miles
    7.  Aquariums Within 15 Miles
    8.  Amusement Parks Within 15 Miles

  In play (Japan): introduced as "a new tool for dealing with dense metro areas
  that we'll get into once it's relevant" -- a textbook just-in-time reveal.

VARIANT MENUS (reference)
===============================================================================
  - JAPAN (Large, canonical): "62 questions" across these six categories.
  - NYC (Medium): an "80-question investigation book"; SOME large-game questions
    are BANNED at medium size ("a few questions from our large games are banned").
    Same six categories; Tentacles capped at the 1-mile set.
  - SWITZERLAND (older): "76 questions" in FIVE categories -- Relative
    (= Matching + Measuring), Radar, Photo, Oddballs (task-style, ancestor of
    Tentacles), and Precision (endgame pinpoint, e.g. "nearest hotel's nightly
    price," "upward photo," "front-facing photo"). Coins, not cards, were the
    reward (rules.txt section 11).


===============================================================================
QUEBEC-CITY LOCALIZATION (summary; full guidance in rules.txt 13.4)
===============================================================================
  KEEP (strong local signal):
    - Body of Water / Coastline / Sea Level / "biggest body of water" photo
      -> the St. Lawrence River is an excellent referent.
    - 1st/2nd/3rd Administrative Division -> region (Capitale-Nationale) /
      agglomeration-RCM / BOROUGH (arrondissement). Boroughs are your best
      "same division?" question (as NYC used its 5 boroughs).
    - Museum / Library / Hospital / Movie Theater matching & measuring -> plenty
      of instances in Quebec City; good signal.
  REINTERPRET:
    - "High-Speed Train Line" / "Metro Lines" -> no HSR or metro here; remap to
      "VIA Rail line / Gare du Palais" and "Metrobus lines," or ban.
    - "Transit Line" (Matching) -> RTC bus route (Metrobus 800/801/802/803/807,
      eXpress, regular).
  BAN singletons that give away too much or too little for your size:
    - "Commercial Airport" (only YQB), "Foreign Consulate," "Aquarium" (single
      Aquarium du Quebec), "Amusement Park" (Valcartier) -- keep only where >= 2
      instances exist inside your boundary so the answer actually splits the map.
