===============================================================================
JET LAG: HIDE + SEEK  --  THE HIDER DECK: EXHAUSTIVE CARD CATALOG
===============================================================================

Every card the Hider can draw, by type: TIME BONUSES, POWERUPS, and CURSES.
Curse effects and casting costs are reproduced from the community master deck
(jetlag.denull.ru/en/rules/curses/), with the exact show wording noted for the
cards that appear in the Japan / NYC / Switzerland transcripts. Deck-composition
counts and time-bonus denominations are from the lifack.ch deck build.
For how drawing works, hand limits, curse timing, and the endgame, see rules.txt
sections 8-10.

HOW THE DECK WORKS (recap)
  - The Hider draws from the deck ONLY by answering questions (draw/keep amount
    depends on the question category -- see questions.txt / rules.txt 8).
  - HAND LIMIT: 6 cards (raise to 7-8 with "Draw 1, Expand 1"). Over the limit,
    discard down to 6 immediately.
  - The three card types:
        TIME BONUS -> pads your final score (only if still in hand when caught).
        POWERUP    -> you play it for your OWN benefit (deck/answer control).
        CURSE      -> you cast it ON the Seekers to slow/redirect/block them,
                      paying its CASTING COST.

S/M/L NOTATION
  Many curses (and time bonuses) carry three numbers as "Small/Medium/Large".
  Use the one for THIS game's size. Examples:
     "0.5/1/3 hours"   -> 0.5 h in Small, 1 h in Medium, 3 h in Large.
     "30/45/60 minutes"-> 30 min Small, 45 Medium, 60 Large.
     "0.3/1.5/9.1 km"  -> distance thresholds by size.
     "400/400/500 m"   -> Small & Medium 400 m, Large 500 m.


===============================================================================
PART A -- TIME BONUS CARDS
===============================================================================
Effect: adds its minutes to your final hiding run -- BUT ONLY if the card is
still in your hand when you are caught. A Time Bonus spent as a casting cost, or
discarded to draw, does not count. The DUPLICATE powerup, if still in hand at the
end, doubles ONE Time Bonus.

Representative printed-deck denominations & quantities (lifack build):
     5-minute  bonus  x25
     10-minute bonus  x15
     15-minute bonus  x10
     20-minute bonus  x3
     30-minute bonus  x2

Scaling by game size:
  - The rulebook says time bonuses "scale by game size -- use the right value."
  - Observed in play: LARGE game used 5 / 10 / 30-minute bonuses (Japan);
    MEDIUM game used 3 / 6 / 9-minute bonuses (NYC).
  - Practically: a bigger game uses bigger denominations. If you use a printed
    deck with fixed values, agree a multiplier per size, or let a deck app scale
    them. For the club's Small/Medium Quebec game, keep bonuses modest (single-
    digit to ~15 min) so one lucky card can't dwarf real hiding skill.


===============================================================================
PART B -- POWERUP CARDS   (played for the Hider's own benefit)
===============================================================================
Representative quantities (lifack build) shown in [brackets].

1. VETO                                            [x4]
   Effect: Play INSTEAD of answering a question. The Seekers get NO answer. The
   question still counts as "asked" (they can't immediately re-ask it for free),
   and you draw NO cards for it.
   When: during the answer window of a question.

2. RANDOMIZE QUESTION                              [x4]
   Effect: Play INSTEAD of answering. The Seekers must instead pick a RANDOM
   un-asked question from the SAME category, which you then answer. The original
   question does not count as asked.
   When: during the answer window. (Used to dodge a devastating specific
   question while still "answering" the category.)

3. DISCARD 1, DRAW 2                               [x4]
   Effect: Discard 1 card from hand, draw 2 from the deck. Net +1 card and a
   chance to dig for curses/bonuses.
   When: anytime (you must have a card to discard).

4. DISCARD 2, DRAW 3                               [x4]
   Effect: Discard 2 cards, draw 3. Net +1 and deeper digging.
   When: anytime (need 2 cards to discard).

5. DRAW 1, EXPAND 1                                [x2]
   Effect: Draw 1 card AND permanently raise your max hand size by 1 (to 7; to 8
   if you play this twice).
   When: anytime.

6. DUPLICATE (Duplicate Another Card)              [x2]
   Effect: Copy any OTHER card currently in your hand; the original stays. If a
   Duplicate is still in your hand at the end of your run, it DOUBLES one Time
   Bonus in hand. (Used in NYC to copy the Mediocre Travel Agent curse and hit
   the Seekers with it twice.)
   When: anytime.

7. MOVE                                            [x1]
   Effect: Relocate. You get the game's hiding time (by size) to travel to a NEW
   hiding zone at a DIFFERENT station. Your run timer PAUSES while you relocate;
   the Seekers must STAY PUT. Afterward you discard your ENTIRE hand and must
   tell the Seekers your ORIGINAL station.
   When: anytime EXCEPT the endgame (you cannot relocate once Seekers arrive).

  Also included in a full deck: ~25 BLANK cards (for house-ruling / custom cards).

PART C -- CURSE CARDS   (cast ON the Seekers; each has a casting cost)
===============================================================================
The 24 curses of the master Home Game deck. Naming: the printed/community deck
calls them "The ___"; the YouTube show says "Curse of the ___" (both names given
where the show used the card). Effects and costs are verbatim from the master
list; the S/M/L numbers embedded in some effects scale by game size.

--- PHOTO / MEDIA MATCH RACES -------------------------------------------------

1. THE LUXURY CAR
   Effect: "Take a photo of a car. The seekers must take a photo of a more
   expensive car before asking another question."
   Casting cost: A photo of a car.

2. THE ZOOLOGIST
   Effect: "Take a photo of a wild fish, bird, mammal, reptile, amphibian or
   bug. The seeker(s) must take a picture of a wild animal in the same category
   before asking another question."
   Casting cost: A photo of an animal.

3. THE BIRD GUIDE
   Effect: "You have one chance to film a bird for as long as possible. Up to
   5/10/15 minutes straight; if at any point the bird leaves the frame your
   timer is stopped. The seekers must then film a bird for the same amount of
   time or longer."
   Casting cost: Film a bird.

--- MOVEMENT / STEP HANDICAPS (timed) -----------------------------------------

4. THE GAMBLER'S FEET   (show: "Curse of the Gambler's Feet")
   Effect: "For the next 20/40/60 minutes seekers must roll a die before they
   take any steps in any direction. They may take that many steps before rolling
   again."
   Casting cost: "Roll a die; if it's an even number this curse has no effect."
   (In Japan the Hider needed an odd roll to land it -- and did.)

5. THE RIGHT TURN
   Effect: "For the next 20/40/60 minutes the seekers can only turn right at any
   street intersection. If at any point they find themselves in a dead end where
   they cannot continue forward or turn right for another 300 m they must do a
   full 180."
   Casting cost: Discard a card.

--- BLOCK-BEFORE-NEXT-QUESTION TASKS ------------------------------------------

6. THE JAMMED DOOR   (show: "Curse of the Jammed Door")
   Effect: "For the next 0.5/1/3 hours, whenever the seeker(s) want to pass
   through a doorway into a building, business, train, or other vehicle they
   must first roll 2 dice. If they do not roll a 7 or higher they cannot enter
   that space." (Show adds: any given doorway can be re-attempted after 15 min.)
   Casting cost: Discard 2 cards.

7. THE LEMON PHYLACTERY   (show: "Curse of the Lemon Phylactery")
   Effect: "Before asking another question the seeker(s) must each find a lemon
   and affix it to the outermost layer of their clothes or skin. If at any point
   one of these lemons is no longer touching a seeker you are awarded 30/45/60
   minutes."
   Casting cost: Discard a powerup card.

8. THE RANSOM NOTE   (show: "Curse of the Ransom Note")
   Effect: "The next question that the seekers ask must be composed of words and
   letters cut out of any printed material. The question must be coherent and
   include at least 5 words."
   Casting cost: Spell out "Ransom Note" as a ransom note (without using this card).

9. THE CAIRN   (show: "Curse of the Cairn")
   Effect: "You have one attempt to stack as many rocks on top of each other as
   you can in a freestanding tower. Each rock may only touch one other rock.
   Once you add a rock it may not be removed; before adding another, the tower
   must stand for at least 5 seconds. If any rock other than the base touches
   the ground, your tower has fallen. [The seekers must then build a tower of
   the same number of rocks before asking another question.]"
   Casting cost: Build a rock tower.

10. THE DISTANT CUISINE   (show: "Curse of the Distant Cuisine")
    Effect: "Find a restaurant within your zone that explicitly serves food from
    a specific foreign country. The seekers must visit a restaurant serving food
    from a country that is equal or greater distance away before asking another
    question."
    Casting cost: You must be at the restaurant.

11. THE IMPRESSIONABLE CONSUMER   (show: "Curse of the Impressionable Consumer")
    Effect: "Seekers must enter and gain admission (if applicable) to a location
    or buy a product that they saw an advertisement for before asking another
    question. The advertisement must be found out in the world and must be at
    least 30 m from the product or location itself."
    Casting cost: The seekers' next question is free.

12. THE HIDDEN HANGMAN
    Effect: "Before asking another question or boarding another form of
    transportation, the seeker(s) must beat the hider(s) in a game of hangman."
    Casting cost: Discard 2 cards.

13. THE ENDLESS TUMBLE
    Effect: "Seekers must roll a die at least 30 m and have it land on a 5 or a
    6 before they can ask another question. The die must roll the full distance,
    unaided, using only the momentum from the initial throw and gravity."
    Casting cost: "Roll a die; if it's 5 or 6 this card has no effect."

14. THE LABYRINTH
    Effect: "Spend up to 10/20/30 minutes drawing a solvable maze and send a
    photo of it to the seekers (no internet research). The seekers must solve
    the maze before asking another question."
    Casting cost: Draw a maze.

--- BLOCK-BEFORE-TRANSPORT / ROUTE INTERFERENCE -------------------------------

15. THE UNGUIDED TOURIST   (show: "Curse of the Unguided Tourist")
    Effect: "Send the seeker(s) an unzoomed Google Street View image from a
    street within 150 m of where they are now. The shot must be parallel to the
    horizon and include at least one human-built structure other than a road.
    Without using the internet for research, they must find what you sent them
    in real life before they can use transportation or ask another question."
    Casting cost: The seeker(s) must be outside.

16. THE U-TURN
    Effect: "Seeker(s) must disembark their current mode of transportation at
    the next station (as long as that station is served by another form of
    transit in the next 0.5/0.5/1 hours)."
    Casting cost: The seekers must be heading the wrong way.

17. THE URBAN EXPLORER
    Effect: "For the rest of the run, seekers cannot ask questions when they are
    on transit or in a train station."
    Casting cost: Discard 2 cards.

18. THE BRIDGE TROLL
    Effect: "The seekers must ask their next question from under a bridge."
    Casting cost: The seekers must be at least 0.3/1.5/9.1 km from you.

--- REDIRECT / VACATION -------------------------------------------------------

19. THE MEDIOCRE TRAVEL AGENT   (show: "Curse of the Mediocre Travel Agent")
    Effect: "Choose any publicly-accessible place within 400/400/500 m of the
    seeker(s)' current location. They must go there and spend at least 5/5/10
    minutes there before asking another question. They must send you at least
    three photos of them enjoying their vacation, and procure an object to bring
    you as a souvenir."
    Casting cost: Their vacation destination must be FURTHER from you than their
    current location. (This constraint leaks which direction you AREN'T -- the
    show flags it as the balancing information cost.)

--- QUESTION / CATEGORY DENIAL ------------------------------------------------

20. THE DRAINED BRAIN   (show: "Curse of the Drained Brain")
    Effect: "Choose three questions in different categories. The seekers cannot
    ask those questions for the rest of the run."
    Casting cost: Discard your hand.
    (Used in both Japan and NYC to permanently ban high-value questions -- e.g.
    banning an easy half-mile Thermometer, or endgame pinpoint photos.)

21. SPOTTY MEMORY
    Effect: "For the rest of the run, one random category of questions will be
    disabled at all times. After this curse is played the seeker(s) must roll a
    die to determine the category to be disabled."
    Casting cost: Discard a time bonus card.

--- ENCUMBRANCE / TEAMMATE ----------------------------------------------------

22. WATER WEIGHT
    Effect: "Seeker(s) must acquire and carry at least 2 liters of liquid per
    seeker for the rest of your run. They cannot ask another question until they
    have acquired the liquid."
    Casting cost: The seekers must be within 300 m of a body of water.

23. THE EGG PARTNER   (show: "Curse of the Egg Partner")
    Effect: "Seeker(s) must acquire an egg before asking another question. This
    egg is now treated as an official team member of the seekers. If any team
    members are abandoned or killed (defined as a crack in the egg's case)
    before the end of your run, you are awarded an extra 30/45/60 minutes."
    Casting cost: Discard two cards.

--- DECK / DRAW ECONOMY -------------------------------------------------------

24. THE OVERFLOWING CHALICE
    Effect: "For the next three questions, you may draw (not keep) an additional
    card when drawing from the hider deck."
    Casting cost: Discard a card.
    (A self-buffing "curse" -- it improves YOUR draws rather than hurting the
    Seekers directly; balanced by costing a card and only lasting 3 questions.)

PART D -- CURSE CASTING RULES (recap)
===============================================================================
  - Pay the casting cost to cast. Costs are: discard cards (hand / N cards /
    a time bonus / a powerup); OR a board condition (seekers a certain distance
    away, outside, heading wrong way, near water); OR a die roll that may fizzle
    the curse (Gambler's Feet, Endless Tumble).
  - ONE question/transit-BLOCKING curse active at a time (don't stack blockers).
  - Curses MAY be cast during the endgame. Timing is the Hider's main weapon:
    cast right as Seekers board a train, exit a station, or commit to a route.
  - The MOVE powerup is the only card that cannot be used in the endgame.

PART E -- HISTORICAL VARIANT: SWITZERLAND COIN / CURSE-DICE CURSES
===============================================================================
Before the card deck, the Switzerland season used COINS + CURSE DICE (see
rules.txt section 11). Answering questions paid coins; the Hider spent 50 coins
per die (100 = 2 dice, 150 = 3 dice, ...), summed the dice, and the higher the
total the better the curse received (the Hider did NOT choose it). Two curses
seen in that episode, verbatim:

  WILLIAM TELL CURSE (rolled a 7 on two dice -- a low result):
    "Before you ask your next question, you must knock an apple off your
     partner's head from a distance of 15 feet. If you fail, you must wait five
     minutes before trying again."

  SWISS CLOCK (a "question" curse):
    "Before you ask your next question, you must clap 15 seconds after your
     other team member starts a timer, within 0.5 seconds, on each side of 15
     seconds. If you fail, you must wait at least 10 minutes before re-attempting."

This system is superseded by the card deck; documented here for completeness.

PART F -- NOTES FOR THE CIA CLUB (Quebec / Universite Laval)
===============================================================================
  - Use a deck app/builder (lifack.ch "Your Deck", or the denull tools) so draw/
    keep, hand limit, and casting costs are enforced automatically on the day.
  - Card feasibility check for a Small/Medium Quebec game -- most curses work as
    written; watch these:
      * The Lemon Phylactery / Egg Partner / Water Weight need a shop nearby
        (lemons, eggs, 2 L water) -- fine in a city, harder in sparse zones.
      * Bird Guide / Zoologist depend on wildlife being around -- seasonal in
        autumn; keep but expect long attempts.
      * The Cairn / Endless Tumble / Gambler's Feet need loose rocks / open
        ground / dice -- bring dice (rules.txt materials).
      * The Unguided Tourist assumes Google Street View coverage -- good in
        Quebec City.
    If a curse is impractical for your zone, house-rule it out of the deck before
    play (that is exactly why the deck ships with blank/optional cards).
  - Scale time bonuses down for a short game so a lucky draw doesn't outweigh
    actual hiding skill (see Part A).
