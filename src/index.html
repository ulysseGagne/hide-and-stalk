Rules

The stalkers find the hider before question 7. The hider stays hidden.

  1. Discord call. Everyone on the team stays in the call for the whole game.
  2. One hider gets 10 minutes to hide. After that, a new question every 5 minutes.
  3. The hider walks. Never runs. Stalkers may run.
  4. Hide near a path. Within 10–20 m of a sidewalk, bike path, hallway or trail, where people on it could see you.
  5. No tunnels.
  6. Nothing to open. No hiding behind doors, in lockers or in bins: stalkers never open anything.
  7. Answer truthfully. Every question, every time.
  8. Found? The hider shows their code and the stalkers win. If question 7 would come first, the hider wins.
